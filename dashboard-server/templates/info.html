What the scores mean

Takeover performance is summarised as nine principal components (PC1–PC9). Each is a standardised score: roughly, 0 is an average takeover in the baseline, positive and negative mean "more" or "less" of whatever that component measures. Scores are only comparable within the same scenario preset.

Where these come from. The 41 underlying metrics and the PC weights follow Li et al. (2023), A Human-Centered Comprehensive Measure of Take-Over Performance Based on Multiple Objective Metrics. Where the paper is silent we apply documented assumptions; see the metric audit in the repository (docs/hcmtp-metric-calculations.md). Results are preliminary and standardised against the calibration baseline.
